How to use this plan — clicking, dragging, and what each status means
Click a course to change its status. It cycles Planned → Registered → In progress → Completed → Retaking → Withdrew → Transferred, then back to Planned.
Drag a course onto another term to move it. Everything saves automatically on this device.
Registered means you're enrolled for a term that hasn't started yet; In progress is the term you're in now. Importing your transcript sets these for you.
Transferred / credited is for requirements you met with transfer credit or PCC / non-matriculating courses — they count toward your credits and unlock prerequisites without taking a seat.
